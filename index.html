About

I build tools that help researchers and public institutions make sense of complex information.

I'm a Data Scientist at the American Institutes for Research and a graduate student at Georgetown's McCourt School of Public Policy. Most of my work lives where messy qualitative data, AI, and public-interest research meet. Lately, that has meant designing AI-assisted pipelines that help teams analyze interviews and documents at scale without giving up rigor or transparency.

On the civic-tech side, I work with Professor Chris Warshaw's Dynamic Democracy project at Georgetown, building open tools and datasets that make political and election data easier to use for researchers, journalists, and the public. Through Georgetown's Massive Data Institute, I work with Professor Jake Pasner on a platform that lets users explore federal System of Records Notices (SORNs) and see how personal data moves between government agencies, surfacing cross-agency data-sharing practices and governance gaps.

AI-Assisted Qualitative Research

Pipelines that transcribe, code, and synthesize interview and focus-group data at scale, with human-in-the-loop review and auditable outputs.

Responsible AI in Government

Governance frameworks, adoption strategy, and training that help public institutions use AI transparently and accountably.

Civic Tech & Open Data

Public-interest tools and datasets: election-data infrastructure, policy dashboards, and maps of government data flows.

Knowledge & Document Systems

Search, retrieval, and document-synthesis systems that make institutional knowledge and complex records easier to navigate.
